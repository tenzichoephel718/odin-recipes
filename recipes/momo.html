Tibetan Beef Momo

Delicious and easy to make!

Traditionally, the Tibetan beef momo is seen as the staple Tibetan national dish. Although, historically this dish - similar to many dishes or cusines found around the world - was taken from elsewhere and reinvented to fit the Tibetan palette.

Ingredients

  • Onions
  • Scallions
  • All purpose flour
  • Ground Beef
  • Seasoning

Steps

  1. Add the proper ratio of water to your all purpose flour and knead the dough
  2. Prepare the wet ingredients (chopped onions & scallions) into a big bowl
  3. Add the ground beef into the bowl and mix well
  4. Add in the seasoning: Salt, Soy Sauce, Oil, Water
  5. Pour a small amount of meat as a filling into the rounded pieces of dough
