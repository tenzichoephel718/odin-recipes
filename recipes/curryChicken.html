Chicken Curry

Delicious and easy to make!

Chicken curry is one of those recipes that takes a bit to prepare the ingredients, in particular with chopping the chicken into pieces, but well worth it!

Ingredients

  • Onions
  • Chicken
  • Curry Seasoning
  • Tomatoes
  • Cilantro

Steps

  1. Chop the chicken into bite sized pieces
  2. Add the onions in wok and let it fry for 4 minutes
  3. Add in the tomatoes
  4. Add the chicken to the wok
  5. Pour in the curry seasoning with salt
